# COMP90018 - Assignment Rubrics

## Assignment 1 Rubric

| Criteria | Excellent | Good | Adequate | Inadequate | Points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Group & Member Identification** | Group number present. Every member listed with full name, student number, and email, all correct and clearly formatted. (10 pts) | All required details present with at most one minor omission or formatting issue. (7 pts) | Some details missing or ambiguous (e.g., a missing email or student number), but the group and most members are identifiable. (5 pts) | Group number absent, or member details substantially incomplete, making it hard to identify who is in the group. (3 pts) | /10 |
| **Coverage of Assignment Rubric Criteria** | Every criterion in the main Assignment rubric is explicitly addressed. Nothing is skipped; coverage is complete and easy to trace criterion by criterion. (10 pts) | Nearly all criteria addressed, with at most one minor criterion treated superficially or implicitly. (7 pts) | Several criteria addressed but notable gaps exist, or coverage is uneven and some criteria are only mentioned in passing. (5 pts) | Major criteria omitted, or the section does not map to the assignment rubric in any traceable way. (2.5 pts) | /10 |
| **Contribution Breakdown** | Fits one page. Each member's potential contributions are itemized, specific, and tied to concrete project tasks. Workload appears balanced and credible. (30 pts) | Itemized per member and mostly specific. Minor imbalance or a few vague items, or slightly over/under one page. (22 pts) | Contributions listed but generic ("will help with coding"), or not clearly attributed per member, or balance is questionable. (15 pts) | No itemization, contributions not attributed to individuals, or the breakdown is missing. (8 pts) | /30 |
| **Justification & Feasibility of Plan** | For each criterion, the plan explains both how and why it satisfies the requirement. Reasoning is sound, technically realistic, and shows the group is genuinely on track. Within the page limit. (40 pts) | Clear justification for most criteria with realistic plans. A few points asserted rather than argued, or feasibility lightly addressed. (30 pts) | Plans stated but justification is thin or mostly descriptive ("we will do X") without explaining why it meets the criterion. Feasibility unclear. (20 pts) | Little or no justification, plans unrealistic or contradictory, or section exceeds the 10-page limit substantially. (10 pts) | /40 |
| **Total** | | | | | **/90** |

**Notes**

Total: 90 points. The bracketed value in each cell is the marks awarded at that level.

**Notes**

- Level descriptors (graded criteria): Excellent — exceptional quality. Very good — very high quality with only minor possible improvements. Satisfactory — of satisfactory quality with many possible improvements. No Marks — missing or inappropriate.
- Pass/fail criteria (Screenshot, Commit log, Itemised contributions): Full marks — present and appropriate. No Marks — absent or inappropriate.
- Innovation criteria: have no “Satisfactory” level (Excellent / Very good / No Marks only).
- Section totals: Material 14, Implementation 44, User Interface 26, Innovation 16 → 100.
