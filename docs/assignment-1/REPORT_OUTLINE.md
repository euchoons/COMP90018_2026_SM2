## Final editing rules

- Use future tense for planned work and present tense only for verified baseline functionality.
- Do not call relative ranking scores confidence or accuracy.
- Do not call the current classifier trained or image-based.
- Do not claim Firebase, shared missions or a map unless the team has accepted them into scope and assigned an owner.
- Explain why every sensor and external data source is necessary.
- Include fallbacks, device variation and validation rather than describing only the happy path.
- Keep terminology identical across the overview, criterion plan and contribution page.
